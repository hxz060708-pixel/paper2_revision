Conclusion
The resilient fault-tolerant control synthesis problem for IT−2FSs with ROCGFs and SMJAFs has been ini-
tially addressed in this article. Firstly, to remove the restrictions of faulty models depicted by Markovian chain,
a more realistic fault control input model with semi-Markovian jump coeﬃcients is incorporated for portraying
majority actuator faulty models. Secondly, in comparison to previous sampled-data control mechanism, the
intrinsic lag signal ϱ is imported in the control law, which exploited to tackle the controller synthesis issue for
the IT−2FSs. Thirdly, by capturing full advantage of the information of the whole sampling intervals [ tk, tk+1)
and [t k − ϱ, tk+1 − ϱ), we raise a novelty looped-Lyapunov functional, that is so-called dual-sided looped Lya-
punov functional, which is tailored for utilizing the advanced integral inequalities. Subsequently, in virtue of the
Lyapunov functional methodology and decoupling technology, suﬃcient conditions have been established in the
structure of multiple PLMIs for the solvability of the resilient control synthesis. Conclusively, the correctness
and applicability of the raised methodology is substantiated by three practical applications.
Our future direction on control synthesis would be focused on the semi-Markovian jump faulty model with
complex TRs, including generally uncertain TRs (GUTRs) and partially unknown TRs (PUTRs). The synthesis
technology can be extended to several potential engineering applications, including but not limited to doubly
fed induction generator (DFIG)-based wind turbine model [46], large-scale interconnected power systems, near-
space hypersonic vehicles (NSPVs) [1], quarter vehicle suspension systems (QVSSs) [56], high-speed train (HST)
[62], and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) [2,50]. To further improve the practicability, numerous research tasks
are still needed to ﬁll the gap between doctrine and practice. In a nutshell, providing a solid theoretical and
technical foundation to serve the real-world applications is a quite challenging subject which would be deeply
investigated in our follow-up research in the coming years.

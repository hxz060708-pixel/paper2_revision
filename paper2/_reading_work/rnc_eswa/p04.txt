IT-2FSs have been widely preferred due to their ﬂexible structure and practical applicability to plenty of systems

the Hanker-norm-based model reduction problem for stochastic discrete-time nonlinear systems in IT-2 fuzzy
framework. To this day, IT-2 fuzzy control synthesis issue is still an active and promising scientiﬁc research
domain, especially in the practical engineering applications, such as vehicular yaw rate sensor, wireless sensor
networks and modular-reconﬁgurable robots, variable speed wind turbine systems, and so forth.
In numerous scientiﬁc and engineering domains, such as aeronautics and astronautics systems, train cave
interconnection systems, interconnected large-scale power systems and robot manipulations with abrupt struc-
tures or coeﬃcient variations can be accurately constructed by Markovian jump systems (MJSs) [15, 48], in
which the variations mainly produced by stochastic failures or repairs of components, transformation of sub-
system interconnections, abrupt variations in the operating point. Consequently, in the past more than half a
century, research on optimization and control synthesis of MJSs has enjoyed enduring popularity and plenteous
outcomes have been announced, for more details kindly refer to [16 − 19]. Nevertheless, what needs to be em-
phasized is that there exists an implicity strong assumption in the fore-mentioned publications. Explicitly, the
sojourn-time (ST) of the jump signals separately obeys geometric or exponential distribution in the discrete-
time or continuous-time domain, which renders the transition probabilities (TPs) or transition rates (TRs) are
invariable individually. This tremendously weakens its capability to model some complicated dynamics, and
this also hinder its potential application background. To relax such a restriction, an alternative pathway is
developed that introduce a semi-Markovian process (SMP) rather than Markovian process to portray the evo-
lutions among the subsystems. Theoretically, the ST in SMP follows non-exponential distributions, including
Weibull distribution [20], phase-type distribution, Gaussian distribution [21], geometric distribution, and so
forth. On the strength of the variable TRs with “memory” property, that is why the last few decades have
witnessed continuing research enthusiasm of scholars from home and abroad. Song et al. in [22] creatively ex-
hibited a strategy to cope with the asynchronous SMC problem for singularity perturbed semi-MJSs (SMJSs).
Very recently, the Ref. [23] addressed the resilient static output feedback control (OFC) issue for discrete-time
SMJSs with piecewise homogenous incomplete semi-Markovian kernel (SMK). Howbeit, to our best knowledge,
although some reports have been carried out on the stochastic actuator failures (SAFs) with Markovian process
in both of the theory and applications [44 − 47, 59], no publications pay close attention to the SAFs with SMP
on IT−2FSs. Thereby, our main incentivation of the current research is to seek the satisfactory answer for this
thorny problem.
Duo to modern high-performance computer, automatic and intelligent control technology along with wire-
less communication networks have been promoted and developed mutually in recent years, make it possible to
transmit the control signals from the physical plants to controller, which gives rise to the sampled-data control
(SDC) strategy [24 − 25]. In the implementation of SDC, only the information of the system at its sampled in-
stants to the controller is adopted during a ﬁxed sampled interval, which can signiﬁcantly diminish the network
communication frequency and eﬀectively release computation burden. Meanwhile, exposed to the harsh outer
space environment, sampler are gradually aging and the sampled-data periodic becomes heterogeneous in the
process of time. Hence, how to acquire a maximum available sampling interval (MASI) is a pretty hot ﬁeld.
The most popular and important direction is skillfully construct a Lyapunov functional and maximally estimate
its derivative. More recently, the Ref. [52] has addressed the aperiodic sampled-data-based controller design
issue for IT−2FSs by a reﬁned adaptive event-triggered communication mechanism. The Refs. [64] and [65]
have investigated the membership-function-dependent sampled-data control design problem for IT−2FSs. Here-
in, we raise a novelty looped functional alleged dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian
Lyapunov functional to further reduce the conservatism thanks to we only require the positivity at sampling
moments rather than entire sampling intervals.

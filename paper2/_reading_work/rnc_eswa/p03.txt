Abstract
This article mainly presents a fresh systematic framework to tackle the resilient reliable control synthesis prob-
lem for networked interval type-2 fuzzy systems (IT-2FSs) suﬀering with semi-Markovian jump actuator faults
(SMJAFs) and imperfectly matched membership functions (IMMFs), which portrays more features than some
prior developments. The principally target of the addressed problem under this systematic investigation is to
precisely design a faulty mode-dependent sampled-data controller to ensure the resultant IT-2FSs is stochas-
tically stable with a prescribed H∞ attenuation level simultaneously. To better depict the stochastic actuator
failures (SAFs) and cater for the engineering practice more accurately, a neoteric control input model that incor-
porated with semi-Markovian jump hybrid fault coeﬃcients with randomly occurring bias terms for depicting
majority stochastically occurring faulty models is reconstructed for IT-2FSs in speciﬁcally as our innovative
attempt. The occurrence of controller gain ﬂuctuations is randomly, which is regulated by a Bernoulli random
binary distribution with pre-known probability distribution. In comparison with most of the existing sampled-
data control strategies, the intrinsic lag signal ϱ is intensionally introduced in the control loop, which exploited
initially to handle the IT-2 fuzzy control synthesis issue. In doing so, a novelty looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov
functional alleged dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov functional that ad-
equate acquisition the information of whole sampling intervals from x(tk) to x(t) to x(tk+1) along with x(tk − ϱ)
to x(t − ϱ) to x(tk+1 − ϱ) is innovatively reconstructed. Subsequently, with the aid of the structured LKF and
stochastic analysis technique, suﬃcient conditions are ultimately formulated in the conﬁguration of a cluster of
multi-parameterized linear matrix inequalities (MPLMIs) such that the resultant IT-2FSs can achieve asymp-
totically stable. More speciﬁcally, some adjunctive matrices

sundry practical systems or industrial production processes, the failure of sensors or actuators is in-
eluctable, which may leads to system instability and even catastrophic accidents. In view of this fact, for the
purpose of enhancing the security and reliability, reliable control strategy has been attracting considerable con-
cerns in recent years [49 , 51]. The key motivation of reliable control refers to designing a suitable controller,
which maintains the stability and meets the increasing requirements on closed-loop system reliability even in
trap presence of control module failures. To be more speciﬁc, in practical applications, a major reliability
requirement for the considered systems is to synthesize a controller which can tolerate intelligent module com-
ponent failure [55, 63]. Nevertheless, the majority of previous works mainly have two aspects of limitations. One
is that they ignore the randomness of the fault and the other is that the randomness of the fault modeling is not
thorough to some extent. To mention just some of them, Wei et al. in [44] have raised a piecewise-Markovian
Lyapunov approach to OFC synthesis for discrete-time fuzzy-aﬃne systems with actuator faults under a sin-
gular system framework. Sun et al. [45] addressed the problem of ﬁnite-time sampled-data control synthesis
for nonlinear ﬂexible spacecraft, which expressed by T-S fuzzy model with Markovian jump nonlinear actuator
faults. Immediately after, the work [46] discussed the fault-tolerant control problem for the doubly fed induction
generator (DFIG)-based wind turbine model with hybrid SAFs by exploiting T-S fuzzy modeling method, where
the SAFs is regulated by a Markovian process. Regrettably, up to date, despite tremendous progressive eﬀorts
concentrating on reliable control synthesis with actuator or sensor failures have been made, but the research
targets in the aforementioned or other previous literatures are both have certain limitations to some degree,
which may be practical unsuitable for some situations as some scholars have previously declared. In fact, due to
the complexity of controller design, far too little attention has been gained to the actuator with semi-Markovian
jump fault coeﬃcients, to say nothing of controller gains subject to randomly occurring ﬂuctuations (ROFs).
According to the author’s knowledge, no publications on control synthesis for IT −2FSs with semi-Markovian
jump actuator faults (SMJAFs) are available until now, this is another incentivation of this paper.
On the ﬂip side, from the perspective of application, it is still exceedingly diﬃcult to accurate design con-
troller to meet practical requirements due to inaccuracies or uncertainties that may occur during the controller
implementation. These inaccuracies or uncertainties may yield some unexpected errors during the implemen-
tation of the controller to some extent, which may result in instability and deteriorate system performance.
It is therefore of considerable signiﬁcance to introduce supplementary adjustable gains during the controller
synthesis such that the controller can insensitive to potential gain ﬂuctuations, which is academic called as
resilient or nonfragile controller [26]. In consequence, it is not surprising that the resilient control synthesis
with advanced control techniques for numerous physical plants has attracted considerable research attention,
and several fruitful reports have been published in the literature [27 − 29]. When SMJAFs is concerned has
not been fully examined due mainly to the technical diﬃculties stemming from some complicated factors. As
such, herein, we are motivated to design fuzzy resilient sampled-data controller for IT−2FSs to ensure expected
asymptotically stability in the presence of SMJAFs.
Stipulated from the foregoing observations, this article is dedicated to solving the resilient reliable sampled-
data control synthesis problem for the resultant IT−2FSs with SMJAFs. More speciﬁcally, in comparison some
previous faulty models [45 − 47], the promising feature of this faulty model lies in its modeling precisely, in
which the SAFs with semi-Markovian jump coeﬃcients is pioneering considered. Theoretically and technically
speaking, the innovations and salient contributions made in this article can be roughly epitomized in the following
ﬁvefold.
(1) The resultant IT−2FSs (8), in which the randomly occurring controller gain ﬂuctuations (ROCGFs) and
semi-Markovian-type jump actuator failures (SMJAFs) together with the mismatched membership functions
(MMFs) are primitively simultaneously formulated under a systematic theoretical framework, thereby reﬂecting
the system structure of the practical engineering applications more closely and broadening practical application
background and making our research work more meaningful, and meanwhile the special feature naturally poses
numerous complexity and challenges to the analysis and synthesis for IT−2FSs.
(2) Duo to Markovian jump actuator failures (MJAFs) which greatly restricts its potential application
scopes, to break this limitation and better ﬁt for the practice engineering background, the developed SAFs
with the fault coeﬃcients complies a semi-Markovian swap rule. Since the semi-Markovian jump failures model
permits the ST is not only restricted to the memoryless distribution but also can be Weibull distributed and
Gaussian distributed, which is capable of depicting majority stochastic faulty patterns (including bias faulty
model, outage faulty model, stuck faulty model and healthy actuator model). Hence, the considered faulty
model might be more accord with the ever-increasing complexity of engineering background.

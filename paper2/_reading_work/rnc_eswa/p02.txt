Highlights
Theoretically and technically speaking, the innovations and salient contributions made in this 
article can be roughly epitomized in the following fivefold.
(1) The resultant IT−2FSs (8), in which the randomly occurring controller gain fluctuations 
(ROCGFs) and semi-Markovian-type jump actuator failures (SMJAFs) together with the 
mismatched membership functions (MMFs) are primitively simultaneously formulated under a 
systematic theoretical framework, thereby reflecting the system structure of the practical 
engineering applications more closely and broadening practical application background and making 
our research work more meaningful, and meanwhile the special feature naturally poses numerous 
complexity and challenges to the analysis and synthesis for IT−2FSs.
(2) Duo to Markovian jump actuator failures (MJAFs) which greatly restricts its potential 
application scopes, to break this limitation and better fit for the practice engineering background, 
the developed SAFs with the fault coefficients complies a semi-Markovian swap rule. Since the 
semi-Markovian jump failures model permits the ST is not only restricted to the memoryless 
distribution but also can be Weibull distributed and Gaussian distributed, which is capable of 
depicting majority stochastic faulty patterns (including bias faulty model, outage faulty model, stuck 
faulty model and healthy actuator model). Hence, the considered faulty model might be more accord 
with the ever-increasing complexity of engineering background.
(3) A prominent merit over some prior reports is that the information of the whole sampling intervals
are aperiodically and substantially captured. In this regard, a progressive looped functional alleged 
dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov functional is 
innovatively reconstructed, which can further reduce the conservatism. For enhancing the 
robustness and practical feasibility of the controller, the subsistent phenomenon of ROCGFs is also 
contemplated.
(4) In contrast to some previous achievements in the literature, in order to overcome the drawback 
of parallel distributed compensation (PDC) and attain some less conservative conditions, the 
imperfect premise matching concept is considered herein, which is depicted by the lower and upper 
of the MFs. Simulated by the Ref. [58], some symmetric and auxiliary matrices are artfully and 
feasibly imported, which can heighten the flexibility of the controller design but also pose a 
challenge to the computational complexity.
(5) By resorting to the stochastic analytical framework and matrix inequality decoupling method, 
sufficient conditions for implementing the faulty mode-dependent fuzzy resilient controller are 
developed by virtue of a linearization procedure. The developed constraints reexpressed in the shape 
of multiple PLMIs, which can be checked by standard software. Additionally, in the light of Remark 
4 that the TRs can converted to polytopic-type uncertainties, another available method is offered to 
develop the finite-dimensional LMIs, which can verified readily by software package too. 
Eventually, simulation results by three practical applications are carried out to expound the 
availability and efficacy of the proposed control synthesis technique. In brief, the current research
could be identified as our systematic attempt to study the IT-2FSs with ROCGFs and SMJAFs 
simultaneously.

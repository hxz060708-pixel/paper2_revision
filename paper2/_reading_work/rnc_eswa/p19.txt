Remark 7. What is noteworthy is that the computational complexity of the PLMIs will be high and even grow
higher with the increase of number of the elements in the faulty set H and the fuzzy rules when synthesis the
resilient controller. With the purpose of designing the control gains, the unknown matrices G♯m(♯ = 1, 2, 3, 4)
is uniﬁed by introducing the free matrix Gm, which is adopted to eliminate the cross-coupling parts and reduce
the conservatism. Additionally, the free matrix Gm is closely related to the mode of the actuator failures, which
is similar to the matrix decomposition in [39], but it will reduce the conservatism while also increase the burden
of computing simultaneously. To further improve the theoretical developments in this article, more advanced
inequalities including generalized free matrix based inequality and canonical Bessel-Legendre inequality will be
adopted. One diﬃculty that occurs in developing the presented method herein is the computational burden
even for existing advanced technique. This is mainly comes from the decision variables, the dimensions of the
matrix inequalities, and the number of fuzzy rules.
Remark 8. For higher level construction, it is a relaxation of TRM, this means the TRM are not ﬁxed con-
stantly, it still follows MJP, which is more realistically. More recently, the energy-to-peak ﬁltering problem
is studied in [40] for SMJSs with mismatched models, where a hidden semi-Markovian model (HSMM) is in-
troduced to cope with the asynchronous phenomenon between the plant model and the ﬁlter model. Tian et
al. [41] addressed the observer-based asynchronous synthesis for SMJSs, where the asynchronous phenomenon
among the system models and controller models due to the limitations of the complex environments is described
by a hidden semi-Markovian process. The work [42] discussed the asynchronous adaptive quantized feedback
sliding mode control (SMC) for SMJSs with actuator failures using an event-triggered mechanism. Song et al.
[22] provided a new systematic framework to settle the asynchronous SMC problem for SMJSs with singular
perturbations, which can eﬀectively remove the dependence on the unavailable system model of sliding mode
controller in some previous publications. Inspired by these pioneering works [22 , 40 − 42], we will explore the
issue of asynchronous synthesis for IT2 fuzzy SMJSs between the system models and actuator faulty models.
Remark 9. Theorems 2 −4 from diﬀerent angles provided diﬀerent channels to parameterize the resilient fuzzy
controller gain matrices. The diﬃculties and challenges of the control synthesis methodology can be epitomized
as three aspects: 1) The synthesis method of the resilient control gains is extremely complicated than some
previous ones since several features are coalesced in one framework simultaneously, which might to eﬀects in the
high computation burden to calculate the resilient controller gains. 2) The selected LKF and MMFs, which can
reduce drastically the design conservatism but it is also accompanied by an increase in computational complex-
ity. 3) The semi-Markovian model is adopted to depict the SAFs, which also leads to the diﬃculty in tackling
the controller gains. In spite of these, our research article mainly exhibit a uniﬁed theoretical framework to cope
with the IT-2 fuzzy-based resilient controller synthesis problem for nonlinear systems with SMJAFs initially.
Remark 10. The core novelties of this control strategy mainly enumerates the following twofold: 1) The asyn-
chronous MFs, resilience and hysteresis are constructed in a uniﬁed control framework, which more can meet
the practical requirements of engineering applications. 2) A semi-Markovian jump fault model is introduced to
depict SAFs, which is more ﬁt for practical applications. According to our knowledge, there are many paths
can be adopted to categorize the random behavior of the actuator faults, such as Markovian chain, nonhomo-
geneous Markovian chain, hidden-Markovian chain, high-level nonhomogeneous Markovian chain, inconsistent
Markovian chain, or hidden semi-Markovian process, or inconsistent semi-Markovian process. Howbeit, most
of the available reports may not reﬂect the random failures more precisely, to eliminate this restriction and
investigate the asynchronous actuator failures, it is worth our eﬀort to make a further study.

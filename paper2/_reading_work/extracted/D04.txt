Exponential Stabilization of Time-Delayed T-S
Fuzzy Systems via Novel Two-Sided Looped
Lyapunov-Krasovskii Functional
Muthu Vel Rajan Baskaran, Subramanian Kuppusamy and Rajivganthi Chinnathambi
Abstract—This paper investigates the exponential stability
and stabilization of time-delayed Takagi-Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy
systems under a sampled-data control framework. To achieve
this, a novel two-sided looped Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional
(TSLLKF) is introduced, incorporating a fractional parameter
and an exponential decay rate. Unlike conventional methods,
TSLLKF provides deeper insights into the sampling pattern and
system states within a splitted sampling periods. By leveraging the
Cauchy matrix inequality, a new integral inequality is developed
to estimate the integral terms involving system states with fewer
slack variables, thereby reducing conservativeness. Through the
construction of TSLLKF and application of the proposed integral
inequality, sufficient conditions are derived and expressed as
linear matrix inequalities. The established criteria ensure the
exponential stability of the proposed T-S fuzzy systems. Finally,
the numerical results demonstrate the significance and practical
relevance of the designed system, while comparative examples
emphasize the superiority of the proposed methods.
Index Terms—Linear matrix inequality, Time delay, Sampled-
data control, Looped Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional, T-S fuzzy
systems.
I. I NTRODUCTION
Nonlinear systems have emerged as a prominent research
area, receiving considerable attention across various fields such
as chemical engineering, energy, transportation and so on [1]–
[3]. In particular, the T-S fuzzy systems (TSFS) has proven to
be highly effective in modeling the complexities of nonlinear
systems. Typically, fuzzy models are represented as a weighted
sum of linear subsystems based on IF-THEN rules, which
are characterized by fuzzy membership functions (FMFs) [4].
Furthermore, stabilization is a key focus in system analysis
and control, playing a crucial role in ensuring the proper
operation of real-world systems. Recently, plenty of study
has been focused on the stabilization of nonlinear systems
through the TSFS approach [5]. For instance, chaotic system
can be modeled by TSFS technique, and the stabilization
requirements also explored in [6]. Based on TSFS technique,
the stabilization of a permanent magnet synchronous generator
in the context of wind turbine systems has been analyzed in
[7].

Time-delays are commonly encountered in many practical
processes, often deteriorating the performance of dynamical
systems and potentially leading to oscillations and instabilities.
Therefore, overcoming the challenges posed by time delays
and ensuring system stability has become an essential task.
These delays are also inherent when nonlinear systems are
modeled using TSFS technique. Due to the prevalence and
adverse effects of time delays, the stability analysis of time-
delayed TSFS has emerged as a significant research area and
has gained increasing attention in recent years [8]–[10].The
main goal of these studies [8]–[10] is to determine the
maximum allowable delay bound that ensures the stability of
delayed TSFS.
Nowadays, with the advancements in networked control
systems, sampled-data control (SDC) has become a prominent
research focus in the control field [11]–[13]. This approach
transmits the state vector only at discrete sampling instants,
significantly conserving scarce communication resources, such
as limited bandwidth. For example, the authors in [14] have
analyzed the stabilization of linear and nonlinear systems via
SDC technique. Also, the SDC has been designed for TSFS
with time delay in [15]. Besides that the memory SDC has
been developed and analyzed to achieve stabilization of TSFS
to deal with the constant signal transmission delay occurred by
the updating signal successfully transmitted from the sampler
to controller and to zero-order hold at sampling instant [16].
To mention a few, stabilization of TSFS under the memory
SDC has been investigated in [17]. Based on the memory SDC
along with conventional SDC scheme in [18], the stability
of interval-type 2 TSFS has been investigated. Moreover, the
memory SDC has been utilized to establish the stabilization
conditions for time-delayed TSFS in [19].
On the other hand, the maximum allowable sampling period
plays a crucial role in determining the performance of SDC,
as it defines a more flexible operating condition [24]. For
instance, a larger sampling period may result in limited com-
munication capacity and bandwidth. Consequently, recent re-
search has focused on developing various approaches, includ-
ing continuous LKF, discontinuous LKF, looped functional and
deriving new inequalities for estimating the integral terms,
to extend the allowable sampling period while maintaining
control efficiency. In particular, looped functional approach
have received more attention to reduce the conservativeness
in the design of SDC for TSFS [25]–[27], since it utilizes
the fully integrated information on the states during sampling
period and relaxes the positive definiteness at the sampling

been constructed for TSFS by introducing the fractional pa-
rameter in the sampling period and it can be divided into four
sub-intervals [25]. As a result, this approach has yielded the

The major contributions of this article are summarized
as follows.
1) A novel TSLLKF is developed for time-delayed TSFS,
utilizing fractional sampling intervals based on the
fractional parameter and distinct with [28]–[30], the
exponential term is introduced in every functionals of
TSLLKF to the exponential stabilization analysis.
2) A new integral inequality is proposed with an exponen-
tial relation based on the Cauchy matrix inequality with
fewer slack variables which reduces the computational
complexity of the proposed method.
3) The sufficient conditions for exponential stabilization
of time-delayed TSFS are established under the SDC
scheme with a constant transmission delay in terms of
linear matrix inequalities (LMIs).
4) In the numerical simulation, the effectiveness and supe-
riority of the proposed method is verified by utilizing the
nonlinear models of stirred tank reactor (STR) system
and Rossler’s system by enlarging the maximal sampling
bound.

V. CONCLUSION
The exponential stabilization of time-delayed T-S fuzzy
systems has been investigated in this study using a memory-
based SDC scheme with variable sampling. By incorporating
a fractional parameter into the sampling period, a TSLLKF
with an exponential term has been developed, which offers
deeper insights into the state information by splitting the sam-
pling period [tk, tk+1) into sub-intervals. Using the proposed
TSLLKF and newly derived integral inequalities, sufficient
conditions for exponential stabilization have been formulated
in terms of LMIs. Finally, numerical simulations on both the
nonlinear STR system and the Rossler system have validated
the feasibility of the designed controller and demonstrated its
effectiveness in achieving larger upper bounds of sampling
periods compared to existing methods.

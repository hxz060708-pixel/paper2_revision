Abstract: Antilock-Braking-system (ABS) has attracted in all fields of automobile applications.
Hence, to offer appropriate braking capacity, different controller types have been
implemented with the required braking parameters. However, in some cases, the
tuning of error percentage became significantly less, resulting in maximum slip rate
and minimum friction. Hence, the present research work has focused on designing a
novel Wavelet Fuzzy adaptive-hybrid Lion-strawberry Proportional-Integral-Derivative
(WFA-HLSPID) for the ABS to maximize the control performance while it applied in the
vehicle. Initially, the signal errors were filtered by the wavelet filter and fuzzy logic then
the controller's gains were tuned by the hybrid Lion-strawberry fitness model. Then the
optimized controller is applied in the ABS application. Furthermore, the performance of
the ABS has been checked with the different road conditions that are snow, icy, wet,
and dry. In all cases, the stopping distance, slip rate, and friction have been validated
using MATLAB/Simulink. Finally, the proposed ABS has gained the minimum gain and
error rate of the previous models.

1. Introduction 
Proportional-Integral-Derivative (PID) plays a vital part in industrial applications due to its 
good robustness, high reliability, and simple structure; it is the easiest control method [1]. Based 
on the system error, the PID controller is modeled, which u tilizes integral, differential, and 
proportional to compute the control quantity for excellent performance [2]. Several tuning methods 
include neural networks, fuzzy reasoning, and genetic algorithms [3]. However, the conventional 
PID algorithms have no noticeable impact and less efficiency, while the techniques with intelligent 
algorithms can attain good effect and high efficiency [4]. In addition, to tune the PID controller 
parameters, soft computing was an efficient, intelligent algorithm [5]. Standard soft computing 
methods include neural networks, meta -heuristic algorithms, fuzzy logic, probability reasoning, 
etc. [6]. Fuzzy logic utilizes fuzzy sets for studying language forms, laws, and fuzzy thinking [7]. 
Neural networks process the data by adjusting  the incorporation among higher internal nodes, and 
probabilistic reasoning makes decisions based on uncertain information [8]. Moreover, the meta -
heuristic algorithm combines local search and random algorithms. The PID controller architecture 
is shown in figure 1. 
 
Figure 1: PID controller 
In nonlinear -based control designing, gain scheduling is commonly used [9]. Generally, 
gain scheduling-based controllers have regulated the dynamic system of liberalized models under 
various operating conditions [10]. M oreover, in-between states, the parameters of controllers are 
scheduled. In addition, the feedback controllers are multivariable [11]. The nonlinear controller 
controls the nonlinear plant connected with linear controllers by switching or interpolation

methods. Subsequently, the linear parameter varying (LPV) technique is used for plants with time -
varying, changing some vector  parameters [12]. In the research based on control engineering, 
optimization techniques have recently attained a dynamic area in this  field [13]. Nevertheless, 
precise optimization methods are needed to resolve the optimization -based issues in the process of 
control [14].  
The optimization techniques like Brain Storm algorithm [15], Fruit -Fly algorithm [16], 
Differential Evolution algorithm [17], Particle Swarm algorithm [18], Ant Colony algorithm [19], 
Gravitational Search algorithm [20], etc., have been used for controllin g the dynamic -based 
nonlinear systems. However, maintain the dynamic system's nonlinear behavior by linear 
approaches. Moreover, several techniques developed recently are radial basis -PID [21], wavelet -
based neural training PID controller [22], hybrid optimized controller  [23], etc., to improve the 
speed control, dynamic performance, and controller's robustness. But still, these problems are not 
resolved yet. Hence , these issues motivated this research to implement the hybrid optimization 
with wavelet f ilter strategy to improv e controller performance. In addition, the function of the 
controller is checked with the ABS.

2. Related Work 
Some recent literatures related to the adaptive Proportional -Integral-Derivative (PID) 
controller are described as follows: 
In aquaculture, the water body's dissolved oxygen -based content is essential for aquatic 
product's growth and requires co ntrol. Zhou et al.  [21] have presented a differential evolution 
algorithm with optimized radial -basis-function with neural network PID controller. The DE 
algorithm improves the PID's initial parameters, and the RBF -based neural network's learning 
ability adjusts the parameters of PID. The results demonstrated that DE -RBF-PID improved the 
response speed, tracking performance, and accuracy. However, the error rate and running time are 
high.

Due to its speed control, the brushless -direct current (BLDC) motors are widely utilized in 
larger applications like aeronautics, consumer electronics, military, and transport applications. 
Karuppannan and Muthusamy [22] have presented a wavelet -based neural training PID controller 
to accomplish the BLDC motor's effective s peed control with specifications. The result indicated 
that the proposed method has the finest speed control under varying load conditions. Moreover, the 
gain was improved ata medium level. 
Yang et al.  [23] have presented a hybrid Particle Swarm  Algorithm and Cuckoo Search 
algorithm to tune the parameters of fuzzy -based PID controller. Moreover, t he controllers are 
designed to enhance the micro gas turbine's performance with varied structures . The outcomes 
indicated that the proposed strategy ha d improved t he dynamic performance and controller's 
robustness. Meanwhile, it increases the computation time, and the experimental analysis is 
difficult. 
In industrial-based applications, PID controller has good performance, but optimizing the 
intelligent controller a lgorithm is required. For that, Guo  et al. [24] have proposed a Fractional -
Order-based Fish Migration algorithm based on Fractional Calculus's theory . The results 
demonstrated that the presented model had obtained better performance. However, the running 
time is high, and particle numbers affect the performance. 
Mohanty and Panda [25] have presented a FO adaptive fuzzy -based PID controller  with 
filter for a novel load frequency -based controller. Moreover, the pr oposed model is a hybrid 
power-based system with electric vehicles depending on the modified slap-based swarm algorithm. 
The result indicated that the presented controller has better control to reduce the frequency -based 
fluctuations. However, electric vehicle gain affects the controller-based actions.

3. System Model with Problem 
An ABS has been employed  to prevent the locking and  wheel slide in sophisticated 
automobiles after the brakes are applied. Moreover,  it is a vehicle safety system ; the 
specific controller is used to regulate the required torque to maintain an optimal slip ratio. In 
addition, the slip ratio expresses the relationship between the wheel's rotation and speed. It is an 
automated technology that operates on the same principles as cadence  and threshold breaking that 
was skilled drivers used with prior generation braking systems. Its response time was  lightning 
fast, making steering effortless for the dri ver. Also, the ABS provides increased vehicle stability 
and reduces stopping length on dry and slippery surfaces. The ABS model is illustrated in figure 2.

Comment on SMCA-25-06-2213
This paper investigates the exponential stabilization of time-delayed T-S fuzzy systems under a sampled-data control framework by proposing a novel two-sided looped Lyapunov-Krasovskii functional (TSLLKF). The introduction of a fractional parameter and an exponential decay term enhances the utilization of state information within sampling intervals, thereby reducing conservatism. The paper is well-organized, theoretically sound, and supported by numerical simulations. However, the following points should be addressed to further improve the manuscript.

The manuscript requires careful editing to improve English grammar and sentence structure. Some sentences are overly complex or awkwardly phrased, which may hinder comprehension for international readers. Professional language polishing is recommended.

The motivation for studying exponential stabilization in the context of time-delayed T-S fuzzy systems is not sufficiently justified. Please elaborate on the practical scenarios where exponential stability is critical, and how the proposed method benefits real-world applications.

The manuscript contains several typesetting errors, such as "mainn goal" which should be corrected to "main goal". A thorough proofreading is recommended to eliminate these issues. 

The meaning of certain parameters in the manuscript is unclear. To improve the readability of the paper, please provide explicit explanations for symbols such as and .
There appears to be an error in the proof of Lemma 1. Specifically, in inequality (7), the final term following the inequality sign is missing the multiplication by the exponential term.

An error is identified in the derivation following the statement “Substituting (14)–(22) in (11), yields”. The terms  and should be multiplicative factors rather than additive terms.

In Example 1, although the authors discuss two different cases (Case (i) and Case (ii)), the simulation result figures for both cases appear to be of the same type, which makes it difficult to distinguish them visually.  It is suggested that the authors differentiate the figures more clearly, for example by adopting different plotting styles, adding comparative baselines, or providing additional annotations, so that the distinctions between the two cases can be highlighted more effectively.

Most of the cited works in the manuscript are from earlier studies, with relatively few references to recent advances. It is suggested that the authors include more relevant works from the past three years to strengthen the research motivation and to better highlight the distinctions and connections between this paper and existing methods.

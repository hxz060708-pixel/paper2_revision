Entire solutions of two certain Fermat-type ordinary diﬀerential equations
by Binbin Hu and Liu Yang
In the study of nonlinear ordinary diﬀerential equations of complex variables, the
characterization of analytic solutions of ﬁrst order equations are of interest. The paper
under review consider characterizing the analytic solutions two certain Fermat-type
ordinary diﬀerential equations. Such functional equations were originally introduced by
analogy with the well-known Fermat’s last theorem. The authors describe the precise
expression form of entire solutions for Fermat-type ordinary diﬀerential equations
(a0f + a1f ′)2 + (a0f + a2f ′)2 = p
and
(a0f + a1f ′)2 + (a0f + a2f ′)2 = eg,
where p, g are complex polynomials. Moreover, it is a very satisfactory fact that some
examples are given to illustrate the existence of solutions for such equations in each case
of the results in the paper.
The paper is written clearly and easy to follow, and the mathematics is correct and
strict. I think that the results in this paper will get to the attention of other scholars
in Nevanlinna theory.
So I am happy to recommend this paper for publication in your journal.

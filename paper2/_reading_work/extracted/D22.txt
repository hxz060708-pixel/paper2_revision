Comment on manuscript 0292
August 18，2024
This paper provides an insightful study on distributed drive electric vehicles, highlighting the importance of accurate tire force estimation for active safety control.
By proposing both model-based and machine learning approaches for tire force estimation, and validating them through MATLAB/CarSim simulations, the paper demonstrates the potential of machine learning methods to offer more precise and robust estimates, contributing valuable insights to the field of electric vehicle dynamics.
This article is clearly written and easy to understand, which has certain significance. Through the comparison of the two sets of experiments, it helps to stimulate people's thinking about improving the safety of automobile driving, and promote technological progress and industrial development. I think the research results of this paper will attract the attention of other control theory scholars.
Therefore, I am happy to recommend this paper for publication in your journal,subject to the minor revision as suggested below.
Some suggestion on corrections:
1. The language, grammar, and sentence structure are still need to check and improve for the better readability. There is some confusion in the language organization, the author should pay attention to the logical relation of the introduction part of the paper. Examples include the loss of articles and verb forms.
2.The practical motivation and the research background of this article are not too clear, the author should address this issue.
3.Additionally, to enhance the research background of this article, the authors suggested to refer to some related references. More recent literature may be updated to enhance the research motivation of this article. 
Overall, if the author can take the above problem seriously, this paper can beaccepted after some minor modifications.

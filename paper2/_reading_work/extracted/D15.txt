Comment on FSS-D-21-00117 
 
By incorporating with the skills of fuzzy modeling method and fuzzy switching control 
technique, the nonfragile sampled-data controller synthesis problem for the ship electric 
propulsion systems (SEPSs) with lag signal and stochastic actuator failures (SAFs) has 
been addressed  in this article. To delineate the SAFs, the control input model is 
incorporated with semi-Markovian jump fault coefficients for depicting various faulty 
models, which has stronger modeling ability. By fully capturing the information of the 
sampling intervals as well as the available information of fuzzy membership functions 
(FMFs) simultaneously, a refined dual -sided looped FMFs -dependent Lyapunov 
functional is raised. Several sufficient conditions are established in the shape of multi-
parameterized linear matrix inequalities (M -PLMIs) such that the underlying fuzzy 
systems can achieve the asymptotically stable . The organization of this paper is well 
structured. This work is interesting and the innovation is sound. However, the following 
aspects should be considered to improve the quality of the paper. 
 
1. It is very complex and comprehensive problem to consider fuzzy membership 
functions-dependent looped Lyapunov functional and  semi-Markovian faulty 
model along with fuzzy switched control strategy, the developed multi-
parameterized linear matrix inequalities (M-PLMIs) seem to be quite complex and 
only feasibility problem are considered herein. And the readers may want to know 
how much conservatism is introduced.

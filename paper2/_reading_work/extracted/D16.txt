Highlights
Theoretically and technically speaking, the innovations and salient contributions made in this 
article can be roughly epitomized in the following fivefold.
(1) The resultant IT−2FSs (8), in which the randomly occurring controller gain fluctuations 
(ROCGFs) and semi-Markovian-type jump actuator failures (SMJAFs) together with the 
mismatched membership functions (MMFs) are primitively simultaneously formulated under a 
systematic theoretical framework, thereby reflecting the system structure of the practical 
engineering applications more closely and broadening practical application background and making 
our research work more meaningful, and meanwhile the special feature naturally poses numerous 
complexity and challenges to the analysis and synthesis for IT−2FSs.
(2) Duo to Markovian jump actuator failures (MJAFs) which greatly restricts its potential 
application scopes, to break this limitation and better fit for the practice engineering background, 
the developed SAFs with the fault coefficients complies a semi-Markovian swap rule. Since the 
semi-Markovian jump failures model permits the ST is not only restricted to the memoryless 
distribution but also can be Weibull distributed and Gaussian distributed, which is capable of 
depicting majority stochastic faulty patterns (including bias faulty model, outage faulty model, stuck 
faulty model and healthy actuator model). Hence, the considered faulty model might be more accord 
with the ever-increasing complexity of engineering background.
(3) A prominent merit over some prior reports is that the information of the whole sampling intervals
are aperiodically and substantially captured. In this regard, a progressive looped functional alleged 
dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov functional is 
innovatively reconstructed, which can further reduce the conservatism. For enhancing the 
robustness and practical feasibility of the controller, the subsistent phenomenon of ROCGFs is also 
contemplated.
(4) In contrast to some previous achievements in the literature, in order to overcome the drawback 
of parallel distributed compensation (PDC) and attain some less conservative conditions, the 
imperfect premise matching concept is considered herein, which is depicted by the lower and upper 
of the MFs. Simulated by the Ref. [58], some symmetric and auxiliary matrices are artfully and 
feasibly imported, which can heighten the flexibility of the controller design but also pose a 
challenge to the computational complexity.
(5) By resorting to the stochastic analytical framework and matrix inequality decoupling method, 
sufficient conditions for implementing the faulty mode-dependent fuzzy resilient controller are 
developed by virtue of a linearization procedure. The developed constraints reexpressed in the shape 
of multiple PLMIs, which can be checked by standard software. Additionally, in the light of Remark 
4 that the TRs can converted to polytopic-type uncertainties, another available method is offered to 
develop the finite-dimensional LMIs, which can verified readily by software package too. 
Eventually, simulation results by three practical applications are carried out to expound the 
availability and efficacy of the proposed control synthesis technique. In brief, the current research
could be identified as our systematic attempt to study the IT-2FSs with ROCGFs and SMJAFs 
simultaneously.

Abstract
This article mainly presents a fresh systematic framework to tackle the resilient reliable control synthesis prob-
lem for networked interval type-2 fuzzy systems (IT-2FSs) suﬀering with semi-Markovian jump actuator faults
(SMJAFs) and imperfectly matched membership functions (IMMFs), which portrays more features than some
prior developments. The principally target of the addressed problem under this systematic investigation is to
precisely design a faulty mode-dependent sampled-data controller to ensure the resultant IT-2FSs is stochas-
tically stable with a prescribed H∞ attenuation level simultaneously. To better depict the stochastic actuator
failures (SAFs) and cater for the engineering practice more accurately, a neoteric control input model that incor-
porated with semi-Markovian jump hybrid fault coeﬃcients with randomly occurring bias terms for depicting
majority stochastically occurring faulty models is reconstructed for IT-2FSs in speciﬁcally as our innovative
attempt. The occurrence of controller gain ﬂuctuations is randomly, which is regulated by a Bernoulli random
binary distribution with pre-known probability distribution. In comparison with most of the existing sampled-
data control strategies, the intrinsic lag signal ϱ is intensionally introduced in the control loop, which exploited
initially to handle the IT-2 fuzzy control synthesis issue. In doing so, a novelty looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov
functional alleged dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian Lyapunov functional that ad-
equate acquisition the information of whole sampling intervals from x(tk) to x(t) to x(tk+1) along with x(tk − ϱ)
to x(t − ϱ) to x(tk+1 − ϱ) is innovatively reconstructed. Subsequently, with the aid of the structured LKF and
stochastic analysis technique, suﬃcient conditions are ultimately formulated in the conﬁguration of a cluster of
multi-parameterized linear matrix inequalities (MPLMIs) such that the resultant IT-2FSs can achieve asymp-
totically stable. More speciﬁcally, some adjunctive matrices

practical applications for further demonstrations.

1. Introduction
Over the last almost forty decades, the fuzzy logic theory has made substantial breakthrough in academic
and industrial communities owning to its prominent merits and eﬀectiveness in handing nonlinear behaviors.
Among several fuzzy control approaches, T-S fuzzy model is theoretically and practically identiﬁed as a powerful
path to approximate complicated dynamical behaviors, and has been successfully applied to numerous industrial
domains, such as near-space hypersonic vehicle (NSHV) [1], large-scale interconnected power system with wind
farm, unmanned surface vehicles or marine aerial vehicles (USVs/UMVs) [2], permanent magnet synchronous
motor (PMSM) [3], nonlinear vehicle dynamic system model [4], quarter vehicle suspension systems [56], and
so forth. More particularly, nonlinear dynamic systems can be reconstructed by a weighted sum of a bank of

can be exploited immediately. Thereby, an immediate result is that the modeling and performance analysis as
well as control synthesis of nonlinear plants become relatively convenient. Accordingly, the theoretical research
on T-S fuzzy model-based nonlinear control has gained rapid development interest [5-8]. Howbeit, the aforesaid
publications are based upon the conventional type-1 fuzzy modeling method, which is unsolvable to handle the
coeﬃcient uncertainties in the MFs. But the good news is, to manage this type of uncertainties, which always
exist in engineering applications, the IT-2 fuzzy modeling approach has been developed in a timely manner by
blending the lower and upper bounds of MFs. Speciﬁcally, the T-S fuzzy model can be approached as a special
scenario when all the lower MFs of IT-2 fuzzy sets are severally equivalent to the upper MFs. Consequently,
IT-2FSs have been widely preferred due to their ﬂexible structure and practical applicability to plenty of systems
[11-14,54]. Most recently, Han et al. [9] addressed the security sliding mode control (SMC) design issue for
IT-2FSs with stochastic communication protocol (SCP) and cyber attacks. Dong et al. in work [10] discussed
the Hanker-norm-based model reduction problem for stochastic discrete-time nonlinear systems in IT-2 fuzzy
framework. To this day, IT-2 fuzzy control synthesis issue is still an active and promising scientiﬁc research
domain, especially in the practical engineering applications, such as vehicular yaw rate sensor, wireless sensor
networks and modular-reconﬁgurable robots, variable speed wind turbine systems, and so forth.
In numerous scientiﬁc and engineering domains, such as aeronautics and astronautics systems, train cave
interconnection systems, interconnected large-scale power systems and robot manipulations with abrupt struc-
tures or coeﬃcient variations can be accurately constructed by Markovian jump systems (MJSs) [15, 48], in
which the variations mainly produced by stochastic failures or repairs of components, transformation of sub-
system interconnections, abrupt variations in the operating point. Consequently, in the past more than half a
century, research on optimization and control synthesis of MJSs has enjoyed enduring popularity and plenteous
outcomes have been announced, for more details kindly refer to [16 − 19]. Nevertheless, what needs to be em-
phasized is that there exists an implicity strong assumption in the fore-mentioned publications. Explicitly, the
sojourn-time (ST) of the jump signals separately obeys geometric or exponential distribution in the discrete-
time or continuous-time domain, which renders the transition probabilities (TPs) or transition rates (TRs) are
invariable individually. This tremendously weakens its capability to model some complicated dynamics, and
this also hinder its potential application background. To relax such a restriction, an alternative pathway is
developed that introduce a semi-Markovian process (SMP) rather than Markovian process to portray the evo-
lutions among the subsystems. Theoretically, the ST in SMP follows non-exponential distributions, including
Weibull distribution [20], phase-type distribution, Gaussian distribution [21], geometric distribution, and so
forth. On the strength of the variable TRs with “memory” property, that is why the last few decades have
witnessed continuing research enthusiasm of scholars from home and abroad. Song et al. in [22] creatively ex-
hibited a strategy to cope with the asynchronous SMC problem for singularity perturbed semi-MJSs (SMJSs).
Very recently, the Ref. [23] addressed the resilient static output feedback control (OFC) issue for discrete-time
SMJSs with piecewise homogenous incomplete semi-Markovian kernel (SMK). Howbeit, to our best knowledge,
although some reports have been carried out on the stochastic actuator failures (SAFs) with Markovian process
in both of the theory and applications [44 − 47, 59], no publications pay close attention to the SAFs with SMP
on IT−2FSs. Thereby, our main incentivation of the current research is to seek the satisfactory answer for this
thorny problem.
Duo to modern high-performance computer, automatic and intelligent control technology along with wire-
less communication networks have been promoted and developed mutually in recent years, make it possible to
transmit the control signals from the physical plants to controller, which gives rise to the sampled-data control
(SDC) strategy [24 − 25]. In the implementation of SDC, only the information of the system at its sampled in-
stants to the controller is adopted during a ﬁxed sampled interval, which can signiﬁcantly diminish the network
communication frequency and eﬀectively release computation burden. Meanwhile, exposed to the harsh outer
space environment, sampler are gradually aging and the sampled-data periodic becomes heterogeneous in the
process of time. Hence, how to acquire a maximum available sampling interval (MASI) is a pretty hot ﬁeld.
The most popular and important direction is skillfully construct a Lyapunov functional and maximally estimate
its derivative. More recently, the Ref. [52] has addressed the aperiodic sampled-data-based controller design
issue for IT−2FSs by a reﬁned adaptive event-triggered communication mechanism. The Refs. [64] and [65]
have investigated the membership-function-dependent sampled-data control design problem for IT−2FSs. Here-
in, we raise a novelty looped functional alleged dual-sided sampled-interval-dependent looped semi-Markovian
Lyapunov functional to further reduce the conservatism thanks to we only require the positivity at sampling
moments rather than entire sampling intervals.

sundry practical systems or industrial production processes, the failure of sensors or actuators is in-
eluctable, which may leads to system instability and even catastrophic accidents. In view of this fact, for the
purpose of enhancing the security and reliability, reliable control strategy has been attracting considerable con-
cerns in recent years [49 , 51]. The key motivation of reliable control refers to designing a suitable controller,
which maintains the stability and meets the increasing requirements on closed-loop system reliability even in
trap presence of control module failures. To be more speciﬁc, in practical applications, a major reliability
requirement for the considered systems is to synthesize a controller which can tolerate intelligent module com-
ponent failure [55, 63]. Nevertheless, the majority of previous works mainly have two aspects of limitations. One
is that they ignore the randomness of the fault and the other is that the randomness of the fault modeling is not
thorough to some extent. To mention just some of them, Wei et al. in [44] have raised a piecewise-Markovian
Lyapunov approach to OFC synthesis for discrete-time fuzzy-aﬃne systems with actuator faults under a sin-
gular system framework. Sun et al. [45] addressed the problem of ﬁnite-time sampled-data control synthesis
for nonlinear ﬂexible spacecraft, which expressed by T-S fuzzy model with Markovian jump nonlinear actuator
faults. Immediately after, the work [46] discussed the fault-tolerant control problem for the doubly fed induction
generator (DFIG)-based wind turbine model with hybrid SAFs by exploiting T-S fuzzy modeling method, where
the SAFs is regulated by a Markovian process. Regrettably, up to date, despite tremendous progressive eﬀorts
concentrating on reliable control synthesis with actuator or sensor failures have been made, but the research
targets in the aforementioned or other previous literatures are both have certain limitations to some degree,
which may be practical unsuitable for some situations as some scholars have previously declared. In fact, due to
the complexity of controller design, far too little attention has been gained to the actuator with semi-Markovian
jump fault coeﬃcients, to say nothing of controller gains subject to randomly occurring ﬂuctuations (ROFs).
According to the author’s knowledge, no publications on control synthesis for IT −2FSs with semi-Markovian
jump actuator faults (SMJAFs) are available until now, this is another incentivation of this paper.
On the ﬂip side, from the perspective of application, it is still exceedingly diﬃcult to accurate design con-
troller to meet practical requirements due to inaccuracies or uncertainties that may occur during the controller
implementation. These inaccuracies or uncertainties may yield some unexpected errors during the implemen-
tation of the controller to some extent, which may result in instability and deteriorate system performance.
It is therefore of considerable signiﬁcance to introduce supplementary adjustable gains during the controller
synthesis such that the controller can insensitive to potential gain ﬂuctuations, which is academic called as
resilient or nonfragile controller [26]. In consequence, it is not surprising that the resilient control synthesis
with advanced control techniques for numerous physical plants has attracted considerable research attention,
and several fruitful reports have been published in the literature [27 − 29]. When SMJAFs is concerned has
not been fully examined due mainly to the technical diﬃculties stemming from some complicated factors. As
such, herein, we are motivated to design fuzzy resilient sampled-data controller for IT−2FSs to ensure expected
asymptotically stability in the presence of SMJAFs.

among the system models and controller models due to the limitations of the complex environments is described
by a hidden semi-Markovian process. The work [42] discussed the asynchronous adaptive quantized feedback
sliding mode control (SMC) for SMJSs with actuator failures using an event-triggered mechanism. Song et al.
[22] provided a new systematic framework to settle the asynchronous SMC problem for SMJSs with singular
perturbations, which can eﬀectively remove the dependence on the unavailable system model of sliding mode
controller in some previous publications. Inspired by these pioneering works [22 , 40 − 42], we will explore the
issue of asynchronous synthesis for IT2 fuzzy SMJSs between the system models and actuator faulty models.
Remark 9. Theorems 2 −4 from diﬀerent angles provided diﬀerent channels to parameterize the resilient fuzzy
controller gain matrices. The diﬃculties and challenges of the control synthesis methodology can be epitomized
as three aspects: 1) The synthesis method of the resilient control gains is extremely complicated than some
previous ones since several features are coalesced in one framework simultaneously, which might to eﬀects in the
high computation burden to calculate the resilient controller gains. 2) The selected LKF and MMFs, which can
reduce drastically the design conservatism but it is also accompanied by an increase in computational complex-
ity. 3) The semi-Markovian model is adopted to depict the SAFs, which also leads to the diﬃculty in tackling
the controller gains. In spite of these, our research article mainly exhibit a uniﬁed theoretical framework to cope
with the IT-2 fuzzy-based resilient controller synthesis problem for nonlinear systems with SMJAFs initially.
Remark 10. The core novelties of this control strategy mainly enumerates the following twofold: 1) The asyn-
chronous MFs, resilience and hysteresis are constructed in a uniﬁed control framework, which more can meet
the practical requirements of engineering applications. 2) A semi-Markovian jump fault model is introduced to
depict SAFs, which is more ﬁt for practical applications. According to our knowledge, there are many paths
can be adopted to categorize the random behavior of the actuator faults, such as Markovian chain, nonhomo-
geneous Markovian chain, hidden-Markovian chain, high-level nonhomogeneous Markovian chain, inconsistent
Markovian chain, or hidden semi-Markovian process, or inconsistent semi-Markovian process. Howbeit, most
of the available reports may not reﬂect the random failures more precisely, to eliminate this restriction and
investigate the asynchronous actuator failures, it is worth our eﬀort to make a further study.

Conclusion
The resilient fault-tolerant control synthesis problem for IT−2FSs with ROCGFs and SMJAFs has been ini-
tially addressed in this article. Firstly, to remove the restrictions of faulty models depicted by Markovian chain,
a more realistic fault control input model with semi-Markovian jump coeﬃcients is incorporated for portraying
majority actuator faulty models. Secondly, in comparison to previous sampled-data control mechanism, the
intrinsic lag signal ϱ is imported in the control law, which exploited to tackle the controller synthesis issue for
the IT−2FSs. Thirdly, by capturing full advantage of the information of the whole sampling intervals [ tk, tk+1)
and [t k − ϱ, tk+1 − ϱ), we raise a novelty looped-Lyapunov functional, that is so-called dual-sided looped Lya-
punov functional, which is tailored for utilizing the advanced integral inequalities. Subsequently, in virtue of the
Lyapunov functional methodology and decoupling technology, suﬃcient conditions have been established in the
structure of multiple PLMIs for the solvability of the resilient control synthesis. Conclusively, the correctness
and applicability of the raised methodology is substantiated by three practical applications.
Our future direction on control synthesis would be focused on the semi-Markovian jump faulty model with
complex TRs, including generally uncertain TRs (GUTRs) and partially unknown TRs (PUTRs). The synthesis
technology can be extended to several potential engineering applications, including but not limited to doubly
fed induction generator (DFIG)-based wind turbine model [46], large-scale interconnected power systems, near-
space hypersonic vehicles (NSPVs) [1], quarter vehicle suspension systems (QVSSs) [56], high-speed train (HST)
[62], and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) [2,50]. To further improve the practicability, numerous research tasks
are still needed to ﬁll the gap between doctrine and practice. In a nutshell, providing a solid theoretical and
technical foundation to serve the real-world applications is a quite challenging subject which would be deeply
investigated in our follow-up research in the coming years.

Comment on ESWA-D-22-07284 
Based upon the HMM framework, the nonfragile asynchronous sampled -data control 
synthesis issue for IT2 fuzzy S-MJSs with double asynchronous mechanism including 
hidden Markovian jump actuator faults (HMJAF) and mismatched membership 
functions (MMFs) has initially addressed in this manuscript. By constructing a bilateral 
looped functional and exploiting stochastic analysis technique, sufficient conditions are 
established in the shape of M-PLMIs such that the resultant hidden IT2 fuzzy S-MJSs 
can achieve stochastically stable with optimal H∞ performance index. The theoretical 
developments present a significance framework for asynchronous control synthesis of 
IT2 fuzzy S-MJSs, it seems to be an interesting study. The quality and originality are 
very high, and the overall paper is well articulated. However, there are some suggestions 
and comments that should be considered, which are encapsulated as below: 
1. Although the language, grammar, and style of high standard, the authors still need 
to be reviewed by a proper translation/reviewing service before submission.  
 
2. It has to be described why this Lyapunov functional method is better or different 
from other methods. 
 
3. The proof procedure of Theorem 3 is difficult to understand and not very rigorous. 
More details should be presented for this proof. 
 
4. Try to set the problem discussed in this paper in a more clear way, write one section 
to define the problem . The difficulties and contributions of the research problems 
need to be further strengthened. 
 
5. Why choose HMM compared to other models? Are there other models that work 
better? please explain. 
 
6. The simulation part is not enough to show the effectiveness and superiority of the 
proposed nonfragile asynchronous control strategy, please further explain. 
 
7. The notation 𝑆𝑛
+  represents the set of symmetric positive -definite matrices . 
However, this notation is not used in this manuscript, please check throughout this 
work. Such as, “… are defined in Lemma 1 and” (Theorem 1) and the equality (20). 
 
8. In addition, the list of references is not in our style. It is close but not completely 
correct. Before submitting a revision be sure that your material is properly prepared 
and formatted. If you are unsure, please consult the formatting instructions to 
authors. 
 
From my point of view, it can be accepted and published in the journal after some 
minor revisions.

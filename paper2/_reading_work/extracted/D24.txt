Comment on ESWA-D-22-07284
This work mainly focuses on the problem of the nonfragile sampled-data controller synthesis, the lag phenomenon of the signal and stochastic actuator failures as the priorities have been discussed in this paper. By using interval type-2 fuzzy systems, the hidden Markovian process and asynchronous control strategy, a bilateral looped Lyapunov functional is constructed, several sufficient conditions are established by sets of LMIs, in addition, three realistic examples illustrate the application of the work. The results and contributions in this work are primary novelties and have practical significance. However, the following aspects should be considered to improve the quality of the paper.
the language, grammar, and sentence structure are still need to check and improve for the better readability.
 
The practical motivation and the research background of this article should be more clear, and the introduction part of the paper should be more complete, in addition, the author should highlight the shortcomings of the existing research results, such as implementation, in order to sticking out how the work will contribute to the research.

It has to be described why this Lyapunov functional method are used in this work, what is the loss of system performance caused by the introduced hidden Markovian model and asynchronous control strategy under feasible premise.

The description of the model in this paper is too complicated. Please carefully review and edit the description of the system and add detailed remarks for some difficulties and key points.

Please add or improve the simulation section to better illustrate the advantages of your work. More detailed description and quantitative comparison are needed in the simulation part.

The description of the theorem should be more detailed in content and logical in order to better cater to readers. There are too many symbols and abbreviations which should be re-checked by the author.

The author should check the References carefully throughout this paper, there are many references which not fit our style need some modification.

Whether the innovatively reconstructed sampling intervals can be generalized to other processing of sampled data, the author can give a generalization of it.

Overall, if the author can take the above problem seriously, this paper can be accepted after some minor modifications.

Comment on ISATRANS-D-22-00600 
February 29, 2024 
 
The authors have proposed a novel control theory and designed controller 
mechanism for anti-lock braking in automobiles that focuses on the problem of 
improving the control performance in maximum slip rate and minimum friction 
when the tuning of error percentage became significantly less. 
Herein, the project team identified problems with the existing Antilock-
Braking-system (ABS) system, such as the maximum slip rate and minimum 
friction caused by insufficient error percentage adjustment. In response to these 
problems, the team designed a new type of controller, the Wavelet Fuzzy adaptive-
hybrid Lion-strawberry Proportional-Integral-Derivative (WFA-HLSPID) 
controller. This controller combines wavelet transform, fuzzy logic, proportional-
integral-derivative (PID) control, and an optimization algorithm called "Lion 
Strawberry". After the design was completed, the team applied the new controller 
to the ABS system. This includes integrating the controller into the existing ABS 
hardware and software architecture, as well as testing and validating it. 
To validate the effectiveness of the new controller, the team conducted 
experiments on different road conditions, including snow, ice, slippery and dry 
roads. They used tools such as MATLAB/Simulink to simulate and validate the 
system's performance, evaluating metrics such as stopping distance, slip rate, and 
friction. 
Overall, this project is a typical control system optimization project, which has 
successfully improved the performance of the automotive ABS system through the 
design and application of new control algorithms, and has contributed to the safety 
and performance improvement of the car. 
This article is clearly written and easy to understand, it has a certain significance 
in real life, and it helps to stimulate people's thinking about improving automobile 
safety, improving braking performance, promoting technological progress, and 
promoting industrial development. I think the results of this paper will be noticed by 
other scholars in control theory.  
Therefore, I am happy to recommend this paper for publication in your journal, 
subject to the minor revision as suggested below. 
Some suggestion on corrections: 
1. The language, grammar, and sentence structure are still need to check and 
improve for the better readability, such as the body paragraphs should be indented 
by two characters on the first line; 
2. I suggest replacing “figure *” in the paragraphs with “Figure *” to ensure that the 
reference is consistent; 
3. P.3: I suggest replacing “section.5” with “section 5” at the end of the part of 
Introduction; 
4. Why should we study this problem, what is the actual motivation for this article 
and the research background of this paper is not clear. More recent literature may

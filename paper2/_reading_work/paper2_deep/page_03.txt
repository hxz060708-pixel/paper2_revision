layer, the actuator failures are described by a Markov process. To reduce unnecessary network transmissions, an AETM is
deployed between the sensor and the controller. Furthermore, the derivative information of the membership functions will
be exploited to develop a region-dependent piecewise control strategy in the subsequent analysis.

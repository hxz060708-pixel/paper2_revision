waste of limited communication bandwidth. To alleviate the communication burden while preserving the desired control

2.3. Characterization of Deception Attacks
In NCSs, transmitted signals may be corrupted due to malicious interference in communication channels. In particular,
deception attacks can inject falsified data into the transmission process, which may distort the original information and
severely degrade system performance.
To characterize the occurrence of deception attacks, letθ(t)denote a Bernoulli random variable taking values in{0,1},
whereθ(t) = 1indicates that the transmitted data is compromised by the attacker, andθ(t) = 0otherwise. Such a Bernoulli
process is commonly employed to describe randomly occurring deception attacks in networked control systems. It is assumed
thatθ(t)satisfies the following statistical property:

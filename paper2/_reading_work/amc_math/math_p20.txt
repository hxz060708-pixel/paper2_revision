5. Conclusion
This paper investigated the global exponential stability problem for a class of Cli ﬀord-valued
BAMNNs with time-varying delays and impulses. Firstly, we decomposed the considered n-
dimensional Cliﬀord-valued systems into 2 mn-dimensional real-valued systems in order to avoid the
inconvenience caused by the non-commutativity of the Cli ﬀord numbers multiplication. Then, based
on the Lipschitz condition and contraction mapping principle theorem, we established the existence of
the equilibrium point for the considered networks. Following that, some su ﬃcient conditions for the
global exponential stability of the considered NNs have been derived by applying suitable Lyapunov
functions. Finally, a numerical example has been presented with their simulations to demonstrate the
validity of the theoretical analysis.
Based on the proposed results in this paper, various dynamics of Cli ﬀord-valued BAMNNs can be
analyzed, including ﬁnite-time stability, state estimation, and synchronization. Thus, we will investi-
gate the ﬁnite-time stability of the following Cliﬀord-valued Takagi-Sugeno fuzzy delayed BAMNNs
with impulses in the near future.

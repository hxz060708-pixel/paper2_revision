Abstract: This paper addresses the stochastic Takagi -Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy uncertainty-dependent static output feedback control and mixed H∞ 
passive performance analysis for a class of stochastic T-S fuzzy delayed generalized neural networks (DGNNs) with additive uncertainties, mixed 
interval distributed delays and mismatched network noise . Firstly, the T -S fuzzy model and stochastic Bernoulli theory are employed to 
approximate the DGNNs based on neural networks  gains. Secondly, the stochastic T -S fuzzy un certainty-dependent static output feedback 
controller with measurable output error is designed via uncertainty-dependent parameters and stochastic Bernoulli theory. Thirdly, the Bernoulli 
probability distribution is employed to describe random occurrence c haracteristic of sensor nonlinearity in the neural networks transmission.

1. Introduction 
Many investigations on the dynamical behavior of neural networks have shown that the neural networks  researches have been 
applied extensively in artificial intelligence, industrial production, digital communication networks and so on. The neural networks 
are information processing systems and the neural networks can imitate the computing ability and self -learning ability of human 
brain [1, 2]. It is well known that the uncertainties and nonlinearities are often encountered in the neural networks. The uncertainties 
and nonlinearities are often caused by the time delays and network noise [3, 4]. The time de lays phenomenon is often caused by 
the signal transmissions in the neural networks, and the neural networks performance will decline due to the time delays [5, 6]. 
Particularly, the time delays becomes one of the important reasons for producing the nonmini mum phase, and many effective 
methods have been proposed to handle the time delays in the neural networks. The complex external environments often cause the 
network noise in the neural networks [7]. The neural networks may be instable and the neural networks performance may be hardly 
guaranteed due to the effect of network noise, and many effective methods have been proposed to handle the network noise in t he 
neural networks [8]. The fuzzy control based on the system knowledge has become one of the important research topics and many 
fresh investigations have been discussed [9]. With above reasons, this paper addresses the Takagi -Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy control 
based on the system knowledge for a class of delayed generalized neural networks  (DGNNs) with additive u ncertainties, mixed 
interval distributed delays and mismatched network noise.  
Many fresh investigations about the T -S fuzzy model have been discussed based on the system knowledge [10 -12]. Besides, 
some stability conditions in terms of linear matrix inequ alities (LMIs) have been derived by employing the parallel distributed 
compensation technique [13 -15]. However, the T -S fuzzy membership functions may be uncertain if the original physical plant 
contains the uncertainties and nonlinearities caused by the n eutral network induced delay and mismatched network noise. Thus, 
the above investigations may be unavailable for the T -S fuzzy neural networks with additive uncertainties, mixed interval 
distributed delays and mismatched network noise in this paper. Moreov er, the T-S fuzzy model has attracted much attention for 
the stability analysis, because it offers a powerful modeling ability to obtain the T-S fuzzy inference results [16-18]. For example,

6. Conclusion  
This paper addresses the stochastic T -S fuzzy uncertainty-dependent static output  feedback control and  mixed H∞ passive  
performance analysis for a class of stochastic T-S fuzzy DGNNs with additive uncertainties, mixed interval distributed delays and 
mismatched network noise. The T-S fuzzy model and stochastic Bernoulli theory are employed to approximate the DGNNs plant 
based on neural networks gains, such that  the uncertainties and nonlinearities in the neural networks  plant is approximated 
effectively. The stochastic T -S fuzzy uncertainty-dependent static output  feedback controller with measurable output error is 
designed via the uncertainty-dependent parameters and stochastic Bernoulli theory, such that the closed-loop system is finite time 
bounded stable subject to 
( )12, , , ,c c L N   and mixed H∞ passive  performance is guaranteed . The Bernoulli probability 
distribution is employed, such that the random occurrence characteristic of sensor nonlinearity and packet dropouts are described 
clearly in the neural networks transmission . The membership -function-dependent augmented Lyapunov fu nctional is designed 
based on the bounds of delays  and spectral norm scaling technique  is employed, such that the stability conditions are relaxed by 
adjusting delay-dependent parameters. The Schur complement and congruence transformation method are employed, such that the 
computation process of LMIs is simplified and computation complexity of solving LMIs is reduced.

Comment on MATH-8993
This article has addressed the global exponential stability problem for Clifford-valued BAM neural networks. Based on the analysis of the existing literature, time delay and impulsive effect are introduced to get closer to the actual situation. This article completed the description of the system model, the existence of the equilibrium point, and then the analysis of the global exponential asymptotic stability of the system. Conclusively, the methodology brought forward herein is demonstrated by practical examples, which show the potency and practicability. This work is well organized and sound. However, there are some suggestions that should be considered to improve the quality of the paper.

It is noted that your manuscript needs careful editing, such as the English grammar and sentence structure so that the goals and results in your work are friendly to the weak in English. 

Why should we study the problem of Clifford-valued BAM neural networks with time delay and impulsive effect? What is the actual motivation for this article and the research background of this paper is not clear. More recent literature may be updated to enhance the research motivation of this article.

There are some minor errors in your manuscript, such as the use of parentheses in the fourth and fifth lines of Eq. (3.13). 

Hope that you can check Eq. (3) in Theorem 3.5 and Eq. (3) in Theorem 3.6 for the description of uik and vjk.

Hope that you can place the definitions, lemmas and assumptions in the second section together to make the article more normative.

In the Numerical examples part, compared with Fig. 5-8, Fig. 1-4 show the system time response with impulsive effects. It is worth pointing out that the system time response curve with impulsive effects has a shorter convergence time. I think a detailed explanation should be presented. In addition, Figures 1 through 8 should not be distorted.

To display the contributions and innovations of this study more clearly, you shouldadd some remarks. Such as remark of explanation in the Sugg. 6.

Hope that you can carefully examine formatting such as punctuation, line alignment, references, and so on.

Overall, this paper can be accepted after some minor modifications.

Finite-time probabilistic stabilization and mixed performance analysis of delayed
generalized neural networks subject to mismatched network noise and sensor
nonlinearity via fuzzy feedback algorithm with measurable output error
--Manuscript Draft--
 
Manuscript Number: AMC-D-25-04496
Article Type: Full Length Article
Section/Category:
Keywords: computation process;  applications of DGNNs;  stability analysis;  delayed generalized
neural networks;  fuzzy Lyapunov functional.
Abstract: This paper addresses the stochastic Takagi-Sugeno (T-S) fuzzy uncertainty-dependent
static output feedback control and mixed H∞ passive performance analysis for a class
of stochastic T-S fuzzy delayed generalized neural networks (DGNNs) with additive
uncertainties, mixed interval distributed delays and mismatched network noise. Firstly,
the T-S fuzzy model and stochastic Bernoulli theory are employed to approximate the
DGNNs based on neural networks gains. Secondly, the stochastic T-S fuzzy
uncertainty-dependent static output feedback controller with measurable output error is
designed via uncertainty-dependent parameters and stochastic Bernoulli theory.
Thirdly, the Bernoulli probability distribution is employed to describe random
occurrence characteristic of sensor nonlinearity in the neural networks transmission.
Compared with previous works, two classes of membership-function-dependent and
delay-dependent stability conditions in terms of linear matrix inequalities (LMIs) are
derived, then the closed-loop system is finite-time bounded stable and mixed H∞
passive performance is guaranteed. The membership-function-dependent augmented
Lyapunov functional with delay-dependent parameters and spectral norm scaling
technique are employed, then the system sensitivity/sobservability is enhanced and the
proof process is simplified. The Schur complement and congruence transformation
method are employed, then the computation process of LMIs is simplified and
computation complexity of solving LMIs is reduced. Finally, the proposed method is
applied in DGNNs to show the application of proposed method.

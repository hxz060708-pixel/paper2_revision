On the other hand, Cli ﬀord-valued NNs are a powerful and e ﬀective model for representing and
solving geometrical as well as engineering problems [17, 18]. In Cli ﬀord-valued NNs, state vari-
ables, connection weights, and external inputs are Cli ﬀord numbers, thus they are extensions of real-,
complex-, and quaternion-valued NNs. It has been used in a wide range of applications, including
medical imaging, robotics, natural language processing, and others [19, 20, 21, 22]. They oﬀer several
advantages of being able to process data that is not solved by real-, complex-, or quaternion-valued NNs
with high accuracy, and they can also handle multidimensional data. As a result, Cli ﬀord-valued NNs
have become a powerful tool for solving real-world problems and have seen an increase in usage in
various industries, with new applications being discovered all the time. However, Cliﬀord-valued NNs
have more complicated dynamic properties than traditional networks, which makes it diﬃcult to study
the dynamics of Cliﬀord-valued NNs in comparison to traditional networks [21, 22, 23, 24, 25, 26]. For
example, by using the system decomposition method, some su ﬃcient conditions are derived in terms
of real-valued LMIs to ensure the global stability of Cliﬀord-valued recurrent NNs in [22]. By utilizing
D operator in Cliﬀord algebra, suﬃcient conditions for the weighted pseudo almost automorphic solu-
tions of neutral type fuzzy cellular NNs with mixed delays are derived in [28]. Based on the Lyapunov
functions, the existence and global asymptotic stability of Cli ﬀord-valued NNs with impulsive eﬀects
have been established in [31]. Some other related results can be found in [27, 29, 30, 32].
In NNs, time delays can occur due to the processing time and the transmission time of data between
nodes. The existence of time delays can decrease the accuracy and overall performance of the system
[7, 8, 14, 16, 21, 24, 28, 32, 35]. Therefore, it is essential to examine how time delays inﬂuence
the dynamics of the systems. On the other hand, there are many factors that may have e ﬀects on NNs
owing to their complexity including delay, chaos, bifurcation and abrupt changes [5, 10, 31, 33, 34, 35].
Therefore, it is crucial to combine NNs with impulses so that the behaviour of the systems can be
as realistic as possible. Therefore, Cli ﬀord-valued NNs with impulses have attracted a considerable
amount of discussion over the past few years, and several important results regarding Cli ﬀord-valued
NNs with impulses have recently been published in leading journals [36, 37, 38, 39, 40].
Motivated by the above discussions, we aim to investigate the global exponential stability of
Cliﬀord-valued delayed BAMNNs with impulses by applying the system decomposition method. To
the best of our knowledge, few studies have investigated the stability analysis of Cli ﬀord-valued NNs
with time delays by the decomposition method. However, Cli ﬀord-valued delayed BAMNNs with
impulses have not been fully explored and are not receiving much attention, which motivates us to in-
vestigate this topic. This paper has the following main merits: 1) To represent more realistic dynamics
of Cliﬀord-valued NNs, we present a general form of Cliﬀord-valued BAMNNs with time delays and
impulsive eﬀects. 2) The system decomposition method is employed to examine the global exponen-
tial stability of Cli ﬀord-valued delayed BAMNNs. 3) In accordance with Lyapunov stability theory,
